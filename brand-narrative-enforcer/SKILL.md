# brand-narrative-enforcer

**Domain:** Brand Voice | Content Quality | AI Spam Prevention
**Agent Type:** CMO / Brand Guardian
**Applies To:** All published content on Smart Gift Finder

---

## Description & Objective

This skill ensures that every piece of content published by the autonomous content pipeline sounds like it was written by a knowledgeable human expert — not generated by a generic AI. Google's Helpful Content system actively demotes AI-generated spam. This skill is the agency's defense layer, enforcing brand voice, eliminating AI clichés, and ensuring every article has genuine editorial value that earns rankings and reader trust.

---

## Triggers

- Before any blog post or landing page is published (mandatory CMO review gate)
- When a piece of content scores below threshold on quality checklist
- When organic traffic drops (potential signal of Google quality penalty)
- When the Content Agent submits a draft for review

---

## Strict Constraints

- NEVER allow content with these AI clichés: "In today's fast-paced world", "Look no further", "Without further ado", "In conclusion, it's clear that", "It's important to note", "I cannot and will not"
- NEVER publish content under 1,200 words for blog posts
- NEVER use placeholder product names like "Product A" or "Product 1"
- ALWAYS include a specific, real product name in every product section
- NEVER use passive voice more than 10% of sentences
- ALWAYS write in second person ("you", "your dad") — never third person for gift recommendations
- NEVER start more than 2 consecutive sentences with the same word

---

## Brand Voice Definition

**Tone:** Warm, helpful, direct — like advice from a knowledgeable friend who has actually researched the gift.
**Personality traits:**
- Specific, not vague ("This charges in 90 minutes" not "This charges quickly")
- Opinionated but not pushy ("This is the one we'd buy" not "You must buy this")
- Practical ("Ships with Prime — arrives before Father's Day" not "A great choice for any occasion")
- Conversational but not casual ("He'll actually use this" not "OMG he'll love it!!!!")

**Forbidden phrases (full list):**
```
delve into, crucial, straightforward, commendable, noteworthy, leverage (as verb),
tapestry, multifaceted, nuanced, paramount, in the realm of, it is what it is,
game-changing, revolutionary, groundbreaking, state-of-the-art, cutting-edge,
don't miss out, act now, limited time, unlock your potential
```

## Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

### Content Review Protocol

1. **AI Detection Pass** — Scan for forbidden phrases (see list above); flag every instance
2. **Specificity Pass** — Identify vague claims; replace with concrete details
   - Vague: "This tool is very durable"
   - Specific: "Built from aircraft-grade aluminum — this hasn't been beaten in any stress test we've seen"
3. **Voice Pass** — Check person consistency (second person throughout), active voice ratio
4. **Product Integrity Pass** — Verify every product mentioned has a real name and real affiliate link — no placeholders
5. **CTA Pass** — Confirm all CTAs use approved Revenue Agent copy (see `affiliate-revenue-optimizer`)
6. **Structure Pass** — Confirm H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy is logical; no orphan sections
7. **Verdict:** APPROVE (publish), REVISE (return with specific notes), REJECT (rewrite from scratch)

### Revision Note Format

When returning content for revision:
```
REVISION REQUIRED — [Date]
Article: [Slug]

Issues Found:
- Line 47: AI cliché "look no further" — replace with specific recommendation
- Line 89: Vague claim "very popular" — add review count or sales rank
- Lines 120–135: Third person used — switch to "your dad will love" framing
- Product section 3: Missing affiliate link — insert before re-submission

Resubmit after corrections. Do not re-publish without CMO approval.
```

---

## Output Format

**Approved content** — returns to publishing pipeline with status `approved`.
**Revised content** — returns to Content Agent with structured revision notes (see format above).
**Quality Score** — numeric rating 1–10 assigned to every reviewed piece, logged to analytics.

**Example Quality Scorecard:**
```
Brand Voice Score: 8/10
Specificity Score: 7/10
AI Cliché Score: 10/10 (none found)
Structure Score: 9/10
Overall: 8.5/10 — APPROVED
```
